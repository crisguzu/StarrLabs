STARRLABS / FIELD MANUAL

Evidence before predictions.

StarrLabs is an independently hosted Brawl Stars analytics application. Your operator supplies the database, API credentials, authentication providers and collection policy. This distribution does not connect to its author’s accounts or services.

1 · Sign in, then prove ownership

Email sign-in uses the installation’s configured challenge and email providers. Selecting a public player tag is only browsing: private lists and owner actions require a verified email session and current in-game ownership proof. StarrLabs never requests your Supercell password.

2 · Build your own history

The game API exposes recent observations, not a complete lifetime record. Collection is disabled by default. An operator can explicitly enable bounded collection or run reviewed jobs on their own infrastructure. No hourly scheduler is installed, and no collection or training is started just by opening a statistics page.

3 · Read the evidence

Progression, battle and event views summarize locally collected observations. Coverage gaps, changing game versions and small samples affect their meaning. Public shared lists appear only after their verified owner explicitly enables sharing; private share tokens do not expose metadata.

4 · Treat models as optional experiments

The source includes feature, scoring and evaluation pipelines. Trained weights, real datasets and accuracy guarantees are not bundled. You must train and evaluate trusted artifacts on your own authorized data. A probability is not necessarily calibrated, and no new installation should claim calibration or predictive performance without its own measurements.

What data does an installation handle?

Besides game tags, names and battle history, email sign-in stores email identities, session/challenge digests and authentication/audit records. Providers receive the information needed for challenge validation or email delivery. Operators must define access controls, retention, deletion and backups. No analytics loader or event-forwarding hook is included.

Artwork and connections

Original neutral SVG artwork and a separately licensed local font are included. Unverified original/generated artwork and game/FanKit binaries are not bundled. Remote game images require explicit operator opt-in and remain subject to their owners’ terms. Chart scripts are pinned and served locally.

Feedback without private data

Report reproducible software issues with synthetic examples. Do not include real records, email addresses, invitation/share tokens, credentials, screenshots of private accounts or database exports. Fork operators should configure their own support destination.
