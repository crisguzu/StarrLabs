# Third-party notices and artwork boundaries

Original code and documentation are MIT-licensed. This does not grant rights to third-party imagery, trademarks or fonts. Review date: 2026-11-09. This is a practical licensing assessment, not a legal opinion.

## Lilita One — bundled under SIL OFL 1.1

`static/fonts/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf` was obtained unmodified from a pinned Google Fonts commit. Its complete copyright notice, reserved font name and SIL Open Font License 1.1 are retained in `static/fonts/OFL.txt`; source URLs and hashes are in `static/fonts/SOURCE.json`. The font remains under OFL, not MIT, and cannot be sold by itself. CSS fallback font names do not include or redistribute those font binaries.

## Local chart scripts — upstream MIT licenses retained

Chart.js4.5.1 and chartjs-plugin-datalabels2.2.0 are served from static/vendor. Their npm tarball SHA-512 integrity values, file SHA-256 and complete upstream MIT notices are retained there. No browser-CDN script is required for charts.

## Supercell assets — not bundled or relicensed

Supercell game artwork and official FanKit assets remain subject to [Supercell’s Fan Content Policy](https://supercell.com/en/fan-content-policy/) and other applicable terms. The policy normally permits qualifying non-commercial guides, guide apps and fan pages; it does not supply an unrestricted MIT asset license. It prohibits implied endorsement, unauthorized modifications and various uses, and may be revoked. Separate fetching does not exempt the resulting site from these terms.

Display a legible notice wherever these assets are used: “This material is unofficial and is not endorsed by Supercell. For more information see Supercell’s Fan Content Policy: www.supercell.com/fan-content-policy.” A disclaimer does not cure otherwise prohibited use. Do not offer paid access/features using these assets without appropriate approval; policy exceptions have their own conditions.

Sources: [official FanKit](https://fankit.supercell.com/), [Fan Content Policy](https://supercell.com/en/fan-content-policy/). Specific asset provenance/permissions must be checked before bundling; public download availability is not clearance.

## Brawlify CDN references

[Brawlify’s CDN documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Brawlify/CDN/master/README.md) permits direct links, downloads and programmatic fetching, and expressly requires Supercell’s terms and Fan Content Policy. Its repository license does not establish authority to relicense Supercell imagery. Existing code/template CDN references are not a bundled asset collection. Remote media may disclose visitor network information; its optional/default behavior still needs release review.

## StarrLabs visual identity and other assets — held back

Existing logos, character poses, laboratory backgrounds and textures need creation/provider/input-rights provenance and an explicit artwork redistribution grant; MIT approval for code is not automatically that grant. Visual approval is not copyright clearance. Unverified icons, third-party branding, photographs, screenshots and QR codes are excluded. A runnable neutral fallback or separately cleared artwork is still required before the public UI release.
